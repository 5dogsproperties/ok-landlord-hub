About OK Landlord Hub

A simple directory of what to do next when you run rentals in Oklahoma — especially around Tahlequah, Wagoner, and Tulsa.

Why this exists

Most landlord content is written for big coastal markets or for lawyers. Operators here need shorter answers: which form, which vendor type, what checklist, and what changes if you’re long-term vs a Fort Gibson Lake STR.

OK Landlord Hub is that playbook layer — not a property management app, not a law firm, and not a lead mill. Just use cases and local notes you can actually follow on a Tuesday morning.

Who builds it

John Ogorzalek runs rental ops in eastern Oklahoma through 5 Dogs Properties. John has owned 300+ units since 2014 — long-term rentals around Tahlequah and Tulsa, plus short-term lake rentals on Fort Gibson Lake near Wagoner.

That background is why the hub leans practical: screening, turnovers, vendors, deposits, and the difference between a residential lease and a weekend lake booking. A Porter flower farm is a separate project — not the focus of this site.

What this is not

  • Not legal advice. Eviction and deposit rules change; verify before you act.
  • Not tax or insurance advice. Talk to your CPA and carrier.
  • Not a replacement for a good attorney, accountant, or licensed PM when you need one.

Content is informational for Oklahoma operators. Confirm local ordinances, HOA rules, and platform policies (Airbnb/VRBO, etc.) for your specific property.
